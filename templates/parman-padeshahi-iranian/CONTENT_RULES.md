# PMPI-MASTER-V10 — Content Rules

## Core

Content replaces content. The visual system does not change.

- No redesign unless explicitly requested.
- No new logo treatment.
- No new typography.
- No new color system.
- No new header or footer.
- Additional slides must be duplicated from the Master.
- Readability takes priority over squeezing content.
- Future projects must derive from **PMPI-MASTER-V10**.

## Workflow

1. Duplicate the five Master frames (or as many as the new story needs, always cloned from these frames).
2. Place the duplicates on a new canvas row. Do not overwrite `2:2`, `2:17`, `2:34`, `2:57`, `13:2`.
3. Swap kicker, headline, body, and key lines only.
4. Update `FOOT_NUM` to match the new deck length (`01  /  12`, `03  /  08`, etc.).
5. Reflow text boxes only when copy overflows. Do not move chrome.
6. Export native Figma PNG at 2×.

## Do not

- Design from scratch.
- Create a new Figma file for a Parman campaign unless the user explicitly asks.
- Copy P001 or the memorial Master into a Parman job.
- Invent organization names, slogans, or biography.
- Put `ایران قوی‌تر` or any English slogan in the footer.
- Recreate Scott Bessent, the logo, or the lion from generation.
- Edit the canonical Master to “save a step”.

## Phrase discipline (when the story uses it)

Keep campaign language consistent with the handoff. Do not invent political facts. If a later P002-style economic story is requested, reuse the already-approved terms from that project’s handoff rather than rewriting the Master.
